About the property

Description property

Dee Gibney Properties are delighted to bring to the market this stunning end terraced house located in the sought-after Bolton Woods development in Callan, Co Kilkenny.

Set on a corner plot with a unique external layout the property is presented in show home condition. It is evident from the internal design that the owners put a lot of thought into the finish of this property.

Property Features:

•Bespoke cabinetry/fitted furniture throughout.

•Feature wall paneling in the entrance hall, sitting room & dining room.

•Solid oak internal doors.

•Polished porcelain tiles in entrance hall & kitchen/dining room.

•Lots of kitchen storage & display cabinets.

•Solid wood floor in sitting room.

•Semi-solid flooring throughout the 1st floor. Tiles in bathrooms.

•Built in wardrobes in each bedroom.

•Landscaped back garden with a cobble lock paving patio.

•Wooden venetian blinds throughout included in the sale.

Location:

The property is in the town of Callan approximately 20 minutes from Kilkenny City and is within walking distance to the town itself with a selection of schools & shops & sporting facilities.

Bolton Woods built approximately 20 years ago is a well established mid sized development with lots of open green spaces and an active residents association.
